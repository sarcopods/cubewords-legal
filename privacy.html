Privacy Policy

English · 简体中文 · 繁體中文

Summary

CubeWords is an offline word game. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Everything you create in the game stays on your device, with one narrow exception at purchase time described below.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address required.
  • No profile, contacts, photos, location, or health data.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking (we never request

App Tracking Transparency authorization).

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting to us.
  • Your gameplay — words played, scores, statistics, unlocked

vocabulary — is stored only in local files on your device.

Data processed when you purchase

If you buy the in-app "Full Game" unlock, the purchase is completed by Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to verify the entitlement and let you restore purchases on your other devices. During this step, RevenueCat processes:

  • a generated device/install identifier,
  • the App Store purchase receipt / transaction record.

RevenueCat acts as our service provider for purchase verification; it is bound by its own privacy terms (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). No purchase data is sold or shared for advertising.

Children

CubeWords contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no data collection. It is designed to be safe for all ages.

Data retention & your rights

Because there is no server-side personal data, deletion is local: deleting the app removes all game data from your device. You may also request any information we could hold about a purchase through Apple's report a problem flow (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Contact

Questions? Write to support@cubewords.app.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will state the new version of this policy on this page and in the settings screen.
